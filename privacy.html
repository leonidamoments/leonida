Privacy Policy - clipfarm (Leonida Moments)

Last updated: November 9, 2026

What we collect

When the account owner signs in with TikTok, clipfarm receives an access token and basic profile info (display name, open ID) for that account only. When the owner signs in with Google, clipfarm receives an access token for the owner's own YouTube channel.

How it's used

Only to upload videos the owner has approved to the owner's own accounts (TikTok drafts and YouTube), and to read view counts of the owner's own YouTube videos. Nothing else.

Storage

Tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer. They are never sold, shared, or sent to anyone other than TikTok's and YouTube's official APIs.

Other users

clipfarm does not collect data about any other TikTok or YouTube users or viewers.

YouTube API Services

clipfarm uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's Privacy Policy applies.

Deletion and revoking access

TikTok: Settings > Security > Manage app permissions. Google/YouTube: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Deleting the local token file removes all stored data.
