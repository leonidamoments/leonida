clipfarm is a private tool used only by the owner of the Leonida Moments accounts to upload short video clips to their own TikTok and YouTube accounts.

The tool is not offered to the public. Only the account owner uses it, and only to post to accounts they own.

All clips are credited to the original creator and are only posted from creators who allow clipping. Content follows TikTok's and YouTube's Community Guidelines and Terms of Service.

3. Accounts

Signing in through TikTok only grants permission to upload videos to the signed-in account as drafts. Access can be removed at any time in TikTok (Settings > Security > Manage app permissions).
